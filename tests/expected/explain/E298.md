### E298: ramsey_model after ramsey_policy

A ``ramsey_model`` statement follows a ``ramsey_policy`` statement. Dynare refuses: `A 'ramsey_model' statement cannot follow a 'ramsey_policy' statement.`

**Fix**

Keep one of the two, in the intended order.

**Range warrant**

The range selects the offending `ramsey_model` or `ramsey_policy` keyword. The keyword excludes options, body, and `end`. If its written file or token cannot be verified, the previous mapped range remains.
