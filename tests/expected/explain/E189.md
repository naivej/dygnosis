### E189: Division by zero after a direct constant equation

Dynare refuses before MATLAB when a direct finite constant equation `x=C` makes a whole left- or right-hand side `N/(x-C)` zero. N is a nonzero finite literal or a single name. Dygnosis emits E189 on the written denominator even when the constant equation comes later. The official line is shortened after the equation number because substitution lists and arithmetic detail depend on the rewrite; the transport already labels severity. A negative unary constant such as `x=-1`, zero numerator, nested division, and other substitution chains remain outside this check.

**Fix**

Change the constant or denominator so it cannot simplify to zero.
