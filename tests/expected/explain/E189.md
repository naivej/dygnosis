### E189: Division by zero after a direct constant equation

A constant equation such as `x=1` makes the denominator in `N/(x-1)` zero when Dynare substitutes it. Dynare refuses: `Division by zero when substituting constants in equation N`. Dygnosis detects this error only for some written expressions.

**Warrant**

The official line is shortened after the equation number because substitution lists and arithmetic detail depend on the rewrite; the transport already labels severity.

**Fix**

Change the constant or denominator so it cannot simplify to zero.
