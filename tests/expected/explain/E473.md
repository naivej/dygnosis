### E473: Non-separable heterogeneous lead and lag

A heterogeneous equation combines a lead and a lagged state inside a non-separable expression. Dynare refuses with that block's equation number and the source line: `In model(heterogeneity=d), equation N (line L):` followed by `Non-separable expression 'log(a(-1)+a(1))'  combines forward-looking variables with lagged states and is not supported.`

**Fix**

Keep the lead and the lag in separate additive terms, such as ``a(-1) + a(+1)``.
