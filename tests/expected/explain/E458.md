### E458: PAC target expression has no defining equation

A direct target product such as v*v has no matching left-hand side in the effective written model. Dynare refuses at transform: `there is no equation whose LHS is equal to the 'target' of 'pac_target_info(NAME)'`. Other target expressions may depend on rewrite and stay silent.

**Fix**

Make target match an equation left-hand side, or use the intended target variable.
