### E233: Estimated parameter in planner_discount

A parameter listed in ``estimated_params`` appears in the ``planner_discount`` expression. Dynare refuses: `It is not possible to estimate a parameter ({name}) that appears in the discount factor of the planner (i.e. in the 'planner_discount' option).`

**Fix**

Calibrate that parameter, or use a discount factor that does not contain it.
