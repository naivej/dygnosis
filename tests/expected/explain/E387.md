### E387: Name is not exogenous

A name in a plain exogenous slot is declared as another type. Dynare refuses: `{name} is not exogenous.` These slots include scheduled and heteroskedastic shock rows, `shock_paths` targets and controlled `endogenize`, `self`/`prev`/`learnt_in` references, and the shock slots of `matched_irfs` and `matched_irfs_weights`. The `irf_calibration` shock uses Dynare's separate `Variable {endo} is not an exogenous.` sentence. A `varexo_det` name gets its own E317 sentence.

**Fix**

Use a plain `varexo` variable.
