### E401: Shock occurs before it is learnt

A written integer shock period precedes the integer `learnt_in` period in `shocks` or `mshocks`. Dynare refuses: `shocks: for variable e, shock period (2) is earlier than the period in which the shock is learnt (3)` (with `mshocks` in that block).

**Fix**

Move the shock period to the learning period or later, or correct `learnt_in`.
