### E111: Shock variance or correlation specified more than once

A shock's variance / standard error, or a covariance/correlation pair, is specified more than once in one `shocks` block. Covariance followed by correlation also counts. Dynare refuses: `shocks: variance or stderr of shock on e declared twice` and `shocks: covariance or correlation shock on variable pair (e, u) declared twice`. A later block may repeat the pair.

**Fix**

Keep one variance and one covariance/correlation specification per target in each block.
