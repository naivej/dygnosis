### E282: Model-local used outside model

A `#` model-local name is used in an expression outside `model`, including parameter assignments, steady-state RHS values, and shock expressions (not initval/endval/histval). Dynare refuses: `Variable {name} not allowed outside model declaration. Its scope is only inside model.`

**Warrant**

For a steady-state RHS, the range points at the written name and the role is the one known when that use was parsed.

**Fix**

Keep the name inside `model`, or declare it as a parameter.
