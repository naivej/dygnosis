### E394: Shock co-skewness declared twice

The same three-shock co-skewness is specified twice within one `shocks` block, even if the names are reordered. Dynare refuses: `shocks: co-skewness of (v, e, u) declared twice`. A later `shocks` block may specify it again.

**Fix**

Keep one row for each unordered triple in a block.
