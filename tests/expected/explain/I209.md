### I209: Declaration has no long_name

At least one `var`, `varexo`, `varexo_det`, or `parameters` name has no nonempty explicit `long_name`. This is a writing preference, not a Dynare refusal.

Each declared symbol is counted once in its scope. Declarations that come from a resolved macro are included. Generated auxiliaries, model-local names, and other declaration kinds are not counted.
