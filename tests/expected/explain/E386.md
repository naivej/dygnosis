### E386: Matched moment is not a supported shape

A ``matched_moments`` row is not a shape the moment matcher accepts. Dynare refuses: `Matched moment expression has incorrect format: {reason}`, where the reason is one of:

- `Variable {name} is not an endogenous`
- `Unsupported binary operator`
- `First argument of power expression must be a variable`
- `Second argument of power expression must be a positive integer`
- `Unsupported expression`

A moment is a product of endogenous variables, each optionally raised to a positive whole-number power, each optionally with a lead or a lag. So ``y``, ``c*y``, ``y^2``, ``y*y(-1)`` and ``y(1)`` are accepted, while ``y+c``, ``log(y)``, ``1``, and ``y^c`` are not. The reason names the first problem found.

**Fix**

Rewrite the row as a product of endogenous variables with positive integer powers.
