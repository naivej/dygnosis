### E027: varexo_det with Ramsey

``ramsey_model`` and ``ramsey_policy`` cannot be used with deterministic exogenous variables. Dynare refuses: `ramsey_model and ramsey_policy are incompatible with deterministic exogenous variables`.

**Fix**

Remove the ``varexo_det`` declaration, or drop the Ramsey command.

**Range warrant**

The range selects the `varexo_det` keyword that owns the first affected declaration. The keyword excludes options, body, and `end`. If its written file or token cannot be verified, the previous mapped range remains.
