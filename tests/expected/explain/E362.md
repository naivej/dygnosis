### E362: svar_identification restriction mixes Qi and Ri

One ``restriction equation N, …;`` uses both a ``coeff(name,0)`` term (the contemporaneous Qi matrix) and a ``coeff(name,k)`` term with ``k > 0`` (the lagged Ri matrix). Dynare refuses: `SVAR_IDENTIFICATION: a single restrictions must affect either Qi or Ri, but not both`.

**Fix**

Split the restriction into one statement per matrix.
