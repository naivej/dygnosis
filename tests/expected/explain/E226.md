### E226: More than one dsge_var across estimation statements

One ``estimation`` statement estimates the DSGE-VAR prior weight (bare ``dsge_var``) and another calibrates it (``dsge_var=…``). Dynare refuses: `An estimation statement cannot take more than one dsge_var option.`

**Fix**

Use the same form of ``dsge_var`` everywhere: estimate the weight, or calibrate it once.
