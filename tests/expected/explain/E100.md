### E100: Optimal-policy command requires a planner_objective

``ramsey_model``, ``ramsey_policy``, and ``discretionary_policy`` optimise a planner's loss function. They refuse when ``planner_objective`` is missing: `A planner_objective statement must be used with a ramsey_model… and vice versa.`

**Fix**

Add a ``planner_objective <expression>;`` statement before the policy command.
