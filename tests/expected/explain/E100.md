### E100: planner_objective and optimal-policy commands go together

``planner_objective`` must appear with ``ramsey_model``, ``ramsey_policy``, ``osr``, or ``discretionary_policy``, and those commands (except ``osr``) need ``planner_objective``. Dynare refuses either missing direction: `A planner_objective statement must be used with a ramsey_model, a ramsey_policy, osr, or a discretionary_policy statement and vice versa.`

**Fix**

Add the missing ``planner_objective <expression>;``, or add a matching policy command.

**Range warrant**

An orphan planner-objective summary selects its owning `planner_objective` keyword. Checks about a present expression keep their expression range. The keyword excludes options, body, and `end`. If its written file or token cannot be verified, the previous mapped range remains.
