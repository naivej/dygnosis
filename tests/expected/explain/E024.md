### E024: Deterministic exogenous with a lead or lag

A deterministic exogenous variable (``varexo_det``) is used with a lead or lag. They refuse: `Exogenous deterministic variable tau cannot be given a lead or a lag.` Parameter leads/lags are accepted because Dynare treats them as fixed scalars.

**Fix**

- Remove the time subscript
- If the dated quantity is state-dependent, model it as an endogenous variable instead
