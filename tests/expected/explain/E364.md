### E364: svar with two of coefficients, variances and constants

``svar`` was written with two of ``coefficients``, ``variances`` and ``constants``. Dynare refuses: `You may only pass one of 'coefficients', 'variances', or 'constants'.` The third name is unreachable at this pin: ``constants`` is a token the grammar does not take, so spelling it is a syntax error.

**Fix**

Keep one of the two.
