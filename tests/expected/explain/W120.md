### W120: Stochastic command with no stochastic exogenous variable

``stoch_simul`` / ``estimation`` drive the model with stochastic shocks, but the model declares no stochastic ``varexo``. ``varexo_det`` declarations are deterministic and do not satisfy that runtime requirement. Dynare stops with "stoch_simul:: does not support having no varexo in the model."

**Fix**

Declare at least one stochastic exogenous variable (a dummy ``varexo`` plus a shocks-block entry is enough if the model is otherwise deterministic).
