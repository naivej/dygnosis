### W120: Stochastic command with no stochastic exogenous variable

``stoch_simul`` / ``estimation`` drive the model with stochastic shocks, but the model declares no stochastic ``varexo``. ``varexo_det`` declarations are deterministic and do not count as stochastic shocks. This is an extra Warning: they accept ``stoch_simul`` / ``estimation`` with no stochastic ``varexo`` at check.

**Fix**

Declare at least one stochastic exogenous variable (a dummy ``varexo`` plus a shocks-block entry is enough if the model is otherwise deterministic).
