### E377: joint prior statement with fewer than two names

A joint prior statement names fewer than two parameters. Dynare refuses: `you must pass at least two parameters to the joint prior statement`. The lowercase ``you`` is theirs.

**Fix**

Name two or more parameters, or use the single ``name.prior(…)`` form.

**Range warrant**

The range selects the joint statement's `prior` keyword. The keyword excludes options, body, and `end`. If its written file or token cannot be verified, the previous mapped range remains.
