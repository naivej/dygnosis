### E058: Undeclared variable in a block that names symbols

A block that names symbols refers to one that is not declared at all: an `initval` / `endval` entry, a `histval` lag, a `filter_initial_state` entry, an `init2shocks` pair, a `homotopy_setup` row, a `shock_groups` member, an `svar_identification` body row, a `conditional_forecast_paths` `var` row, or a `std(…)` / `corr(…)` prior head name. Dynare refuses: `Unknown symbol: undeclared_zzz`.

**Warrant**

The editor names the undeclared entry and its block; Dynare's string is the generic `Unknown symbol`.

**Fix**

Declare the variable, or remove the stray entry.
