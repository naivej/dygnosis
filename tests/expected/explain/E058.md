### E058: Undeclared variable in initval

An entry in the `initval` block refers to a name that is not declared as a variable. They refuse: `Unknown symbol: undeclared_zzz`.

**Warrant**

The editor names the undeclared `initval` / `endval` entry; their string is the generic `Unknown symbol`.

**Fix**

Declare the variable, or remove the stray `initval` entry.
