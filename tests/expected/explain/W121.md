### W121: Parameter used with a lead or lag

A declared parameter appears with a time subscript such as ``beta(+1)`` or ``rho(-1)`` in the model block. Parameters are time-invariant constants, so a lead/lag on one is meaningless and almost always means the symbol should have been declared as a variable, or that the time index is stray.

**Fix**

Remove the time index, or declare the symbol with ``var`` / ``varexo`` if it really is a variable.
