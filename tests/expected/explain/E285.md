### E285: Macro operand type mismatch

A macro operator combines operands of incompatible types. Dynare refuses with the operator-specific sentence: `Type mismatch for operands of + operator`, or for membership `Second argument of `in` operator must be an array` when the right operand is not an array, tuple, or integer range.

**Fix**

Use operands the operator accepts. For `in`, the right operand must be an array, a tuple, or a bounded integer range.
