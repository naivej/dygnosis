### E384: analytic option without GMM

``analytic_standard_errors`` or ``analytic_jacobian`` is a bare flag on a ``method_of_moments`` statement whose ``mom_method`` is ``SMM`` or ``IRF_MATCHING``. Only GMM has the analytic derivatives these options ask for. Dynare refuses: `The analytic_standard_errors statement requires the GMM option.` (or ``analytic_jacobian`` in place of the first name).

**Fix**

Drop the option, or switch ``mom_method`` to ``GMM``.
