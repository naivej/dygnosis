### W112: Negative shock variance

A shocks-block ``var e = ...`` entry sets a variance that folds to a negative constant. A variance is a squared quantity and should be non-negative.

(The ``stderr`` form is not flagged: a negative standard error is squared, so the variance is still positive.)

**Fix**

Use a non-negative value. Recall the ``var`` form sets the variance, i.e. the standard error *squared* (e.g. ``var e = 0.01^2;``).
