### E223: dsge_prior_weight estimated with calibrated dsge_var

``dsge_prior_weight`` is in ``estimated_params`` while ``estimation`` calibrates the weight with ``dsge_var=…``. Dynare refuses: `If dsge_prior_weight is in the estimated_params block, the prior weight cannot be calibrated via the dsge_var option in the estimation statement.`

**Fix**

Keep one form: estimate the weight with a bare ``dsge_var``, or remove ``dsge_prior_weight`` from ``estimated_params``.
