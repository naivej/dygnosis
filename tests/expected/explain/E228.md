### E228: mode_file with estimated_params_init(use_calibration)

``estimation`` passes ``mode_file`` while ``estimated_params_init`` was opened with ``use_calibration``. Dynare refuses: `The mode_file option of the estimation statement is incompatible with the use_calibration option of the estimated_params_init block.`

**Fix**

Drop the ``mode_file`` option, or drop ``use_calibration`` from the ``estimated_params_init`` block.
