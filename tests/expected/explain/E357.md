### E357: svar_identification with both choleskys

One ``svar_identification`` block holds both ``upper_cholesky;`` and ``lower_cholesky;``. Dynare refuses: `Within the svar_identification statement, you may only have one of upper_cholesky and lower_cholesky.`

**Fix**

Keep one of the two.

**Range warrant**

The range selects the affected `svar_identification` keyword. The keyword excludes options, body, and `end`. If its written file or token cannot be verified, the previous mapped range remains.
