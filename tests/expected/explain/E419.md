### E419: endval learnt-in row is not exogenous

A nondefault `endval(learnt_in=…)` row names something other than a plain exogenous variable. Dynare refuses: `endval(learnt_in=...): y is not an exogenous variable`.

**Fix**

Use a `varexo` name, or put the intended assignment in an ordinary `endval` block.
