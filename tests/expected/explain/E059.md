### E059: Name in initval/endval or a std/corr prior head is neither endogenous or exogenous

An `initval` or `endval` entry, a `histval` entry, or a `std(…)` / `corr(…)` prior head names a symbol that is not endogenous or exogenous (for example a parameter). Dynare refuses: `… is neither endogenous or exogenous.`

**Fix**

Assign parameters before the model block, or inside `steady_state_model`. Use `initval` / `endval` only for endogenous or exogenous variables, and a plain `name.prior(…)` head for a parameter.
