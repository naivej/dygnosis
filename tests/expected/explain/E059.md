### E059: Name in initval/endval or a std/corr prior/options head is neither endogenous or exogenous

An `initval` or `endval` entry, a `histval` entry, a `std(…)` / `corr(…)` prior or options head, or an `initval.x` reference in `shock_paths` names something other than endogenous or exogenous (for example a parameter). Dynare refuses: `… is neither endogenous or exogenous.`

**Fix**

Use an endogenous or exogenous name in those slots; assign a parameter before the model block or inside `steady_state_model`.
