### E059: Wrong name role in an initval/endval, prior, or removal slot

An `initval` or `endval` entry, a `histval` entry, a `std(…)` / `corr(…)` prior or options head, or an `initval.x` reference in `shock_paths` names something other than endogenous or exogenous. Dynare refuses with `… is neither endogenous or exogenous.` A plain `estimated_params_remove` row must name a parameter; there Dynare refuses with `{name} is not a parameter`.

**Fix**

Use a name with the role required by that row.
