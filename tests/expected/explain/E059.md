### E059: Name in initval/endval is neither endogenous or exogenous

An `initval` or `endval` entry names a symbol that is not endogenous or exogenous (for example a parameter). They refuse: `… is neither endogenous or exogenous.`

**Fix**

Assign parameters before the model block, or inside `steady_state_model`. Use `initval` / `endval` only for endogenous or exogenous variables.
