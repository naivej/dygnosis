### E183: Complementarity condition has an incorrect form

The condition after ``⟂`` / ``_|_`` is not an inequality on a contemporaneous endogenous variable with constant bounds. They refuse: `Complementarity condition has an incorrect form` (an extra ``: …`` detail is appended when that form can be named).

**Fix**

Write an inequality such as ``i >= 0`` or ``0 <= i <= 1``.
