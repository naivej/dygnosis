### W070: Parameter outside its conventional range

A parameter's name matches a conventional meaning in Dygnosis's range table, and its value is outside that range. The name alone does not establish what the parameter means in your model.

**Next step**

Check the meaning, units and sign. For example, `beta = 99` may mean that `0.99` was intended. Keep the value and hide this Warning if your model uses a different meaning or an intentional calibration.
