### E432: var(log) variable in a VAR, trend, or PAC equation

A selected VAR or trend-component equation, or an equation with pac_expectation, uses an endogenous declared with var(log). Dynare refuses at transform: `the following variables are declared with var(log) and therefore cannot appear in a VAR/TCM/PAC equation`.

**Fix**

Remove the log declaration for that variable or change the selected equation.
