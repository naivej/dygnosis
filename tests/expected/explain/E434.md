### E434: VAR or trend-component equation has the wrong shape

A selected equation violates Dynare's LHS or RHS variable and timing rules, repeats an LHS, or uses a binary expression on the LHS. Dynare refuses at transform with the matching VAR or trend-component equation sentence. A structural VAR permits contemporaneous endogenous variables on the RHS.

**Fix**

Give each equation one untimed endogenous LHS and use only the allowed RHS timing.
