### E203: ramsey_constraints without Ramsey

Dynare refuses: `A ramsey_constraints block requires the presence of a ramsey_model or ramsey_policy statement`.

**Fix**

Add ``ramsey_model`` or ``ramsey_policy``, or drop ``ramsey_constraints``.

**Range warrant**

The range selects the owning `ramsey_constraints` keyword. The keyword excludes options, body, and `end`. If its written file or token cannot be verified, the previous mapped range remains.
