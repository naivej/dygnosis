### E379: corr prior mixes an endogenous and an exogenous name

The two names of a ``corr(A,B).prior(…)`` statement are of different types. Dynare refuses: `In the corr(A,B).prior statement, A and B must be of the same type. In your case, Pie and eps are of different types.`

**Fix**

Name two endogenous variables or two exogenous ones.
