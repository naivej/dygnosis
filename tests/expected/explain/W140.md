### W140: Nonlinear operator in a linear model

The model is declared ``linear`` (``model(linear);``) but an equation applies a nonlinear operator to a variable. Examples include variable-dependent functions (such as ``log(y)`` or ``abs(e)``), products or ratios involving multiple variables (``c*k`` or ``a/y``), powers involving variables (``k^2``), and comparisons. Dynare requires the equations of a ``linear`` model to be linear in the variables and errors otherwise.

**Fix**

Remove the ``linear`` option, or rewrite the equation without the nonlinear operator.
