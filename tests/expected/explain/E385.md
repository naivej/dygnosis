### E385: method_of_moments with more than one filter

A ``method_of_moments`` statement asks for more than one of ``hp_filter``, ``one_sided_hp_filter``, and ``bandpass_filter``. Dynare refuses: `method_of_moments: can only use one of HP, one-sided HP, and bandpass filters`

This is the ``method_of_moments`` sentence; ``stoch_simul`` has its own (**E238**).

**Fix**

Keep one filter.
