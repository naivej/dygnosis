### E103: osr is missing osr_params or optim_weights

Optimal simple rules (``osr``) need an ``osr_params`` statement (the parameters to optimise). They refuse: `The osr statement requires the osr_params statement.` This check also flags a missing ``optim_weights`` block.

**Fix**

Add the missing ``osr_params`` statement and/or ``optim_weights`` block.
