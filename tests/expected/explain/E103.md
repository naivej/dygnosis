### E103: osr is missing osr_params or optim_weights

Optimal simple rules (``osr``) optimise the values of chosen parameters to minimise a weighted objective, so they require an ``osr_params`` statement (the parameters to optimise) and an ``optim_weights`` block (the objective weights). One of these is missing.

**Fix**

Add the missing ``osr_params`` statement and/or ``optim_weights`` block.
