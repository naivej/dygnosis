### E392: matched_irfs periods holds a date

A ``periods`` entry of a ``matched_irfs`` row is written as a date such as ``2000Q1``. The row counts periods after the start of the simulation, so it takes integers and integer ranges only. Dynare refuses: `matched_irfs: dates are not allowed in the 'periods' keyword`

**Fix**

Write the horizon as an integer, or as an ``a:b`` range of integers.
