### E113: shock_paths with shocks, mshocks, endval, or controlled paths

A ``shock_paths`` block cannot appear with ``shocks``, ``mshocks``, ``endval``, or ``perfect_foresight_controlled_paths``. Dynare refuses: `the 'shock_paths' block cannot be used in conjunction with either 'shocks', 'mshocks', 'endval' or 'perfect_foresight_controlled_paths' blocks.`.

**Fix**

Keep ``shock_paths`` and drop the other block, or drop ``shock_paths``.
