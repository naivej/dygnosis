### W131: Variable silently overwritten in steady_state_model

A variable is assigned more than once in the ``steady_state_model`` block and the later assignment does not use the earlier value, so the first assignment is dead. (An in-place transformation that reuses the value, such as the ``A = log(A)`` log-model idiom, is intentional and is not flagged.)

**Fix**

Remove the redundant assignment, or fold the two into one.
