### E268: shocks covariance on mixed types

``shocks`` sets a covariance on a pair that is not both exogenous or both observed endogenous. Dynare refuses: `shocks: setting a covariance between '{a}' and '{b}'is not allowed; covariances can only be specified for exogenous or observed endogenous variables of same type`.

**Fix**

Use two exogenous names or two observed endogenous names.
