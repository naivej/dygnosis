### E181: bind or relax is not an inequality

The ``bind`` or ``relax`` expression is a comparison other than ``<``, ``>``, ``<=``, or ``>=`` (for example ``==``). They refuse: `The 'bind' expression must be an inequality constraint` / `The 'relax' expression must be an inequality constraint`. A non-binary ``bind i;`` is a missing bind (E174), not this code.

**Fix**

Write an inequality.
