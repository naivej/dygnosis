### E336: Excluded equation has no single endogenous on its left side

An equation removed by `model_remove`, `include_eqs` or `exclude_eqs` has no `endogenous` tag, and its left side contains zero or several distinct endogenous variables. `model_replace` does not require this rule.

**Fix**

Add an `endogenous='y'` tag, or use a left side containing only one distinct endogenous variable, such as `2*y`. See [Dynare's equation-selection rules](https://www.dynare.org/manual/running-dynare.html#exclude-eqs).
