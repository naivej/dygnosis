### E302: planner_discount on ramsey_policy with a declared optimal_policy_discount_factor

``ramsey_policy(planner_discount=…)`` is used while ``optimal_policy_discount_factor`` is already declared as a parameter. Dynare refuses: `ramsey_policy: the 'planner_discount' option cannot be used when the 'optimal_policy_discount_factor' parameter is explicitly declared.`

**Fix**

Drop the ``planner_discount`` option, or remove the parameter declaration.
