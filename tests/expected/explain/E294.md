### E294: Epilogue name outside the epilogue block

A name declared in the `epilogue` block is used in the model or another statement, including a steady-state RHS. Dynare refuses: `Symbol 'foo' cannot be used outside the epilogue block.`

**Warrant**

For a steady-state RHS, the range points at the written name and the role is the one known when that use was parsed.

**Fix**

Use the name only inside the `epilogue` block.
