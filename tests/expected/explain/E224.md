### E224: dsge_prior_weight estimated without dsge_var

``dsge_prior_weight`` is in ``estimated_params`` but no ``estimation`` statement passes ``dsge_var``. Dynare refuses: `If dsge_prior_weight is in the estimated_params block, the dsge_var option must be passed to the estimation statement.`

**Fix**

Add ``dsge_var`` to the estimation statement, or remove ``dsge_prior_weight`` from ``estimated_params``.
