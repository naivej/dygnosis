### E382: method_of_moments without a method

A ``method_of_moments`` statement carries no ``mom_method`` option, so Dynare cannot tell GMM, SMM, and IRF matching apart. Dynare refuses: `The 'method_of_moments' statement requires a method to be supplied via the 'mom_method' option. Possible values are 'GMM', 'SMM', or 'IRF_MATCHING'.`

The option value must be one of those three bare words; a quoted or other word is a syntax error, reported as **E001**.

**Fix**

Add ``mom_method=GMM``, ``mom_method=SMM``, or ``mom_method=IRF_MATCHING``.
