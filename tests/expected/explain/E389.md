### E389: matched_irfs_weights tuple repeated

One ``matched_irfs_weights`` block lists the same six-part tuple twice: both endogenous names with their periods, and both shock names. The weight is not part of the key, so two rows differing only in the weight are refused. Dynare refuses: `matched_irfs: the tuple ({y}({1}),{e},{c}({2}),{e}) appears two times`, with no spaces around the parts.

**Fix**

Merge the two rows, or drop the second.
