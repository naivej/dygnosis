### E062: Unmatched macro block

A Dynare macro `@#if` block has no matching `@#endif`, or a `@#for` block has no matching `@#endfor`. Dynare's preprocessor expands these directives at build time and will reject unbalanced control flow.

**Common causes**

- A copy-paste deleted the closing directive
- Mismatched closers — `@#endif` accidentally written for   a `@#for`, or vice versa
- A nested block missing its inner closer

**Fix**

Add the missing `@#endif` or `@#endfor` at the appropriate scope, or remove the stray closer. Each `@#if` needs its own `@#endif`; each `@#for` its own `@#endfor`.
