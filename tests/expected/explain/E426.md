### E426: Removed variable used in an expression

An expression uses a variable after `model_remove` or `var_remove` changed its type to excluded. This includes planner objectives, Ramsey constraints, and steady-state RHS values. Dynare refuses: `Variable 'c' can no longer be used since it has been excluded by a previous 'model_remove' or 'var_remove' statement`. A use before the removal, or after a successful type restoration, is allowed.

**Warrant**

For a steady-state RHS, the range points at the written name and its excluded role is captured when the use is parsed, including repeated macro copies.

**Fix**

Keep the variable in the model, or stop using it after its removal.
