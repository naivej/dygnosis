### E448: PAC growth has a nonlinear written product

A PAC growth expression directly multiplies two variables, so Dynare refuses at transform: `PAC growth must be a linear combination of variables`. Other growth forms may need rewrite before this check.

This check uses the final symbol type after successful `change_type` statements, rather than the list where the name was declared.

**Fix**

Use a linear combination of variables and constants for growth.
