### E221: DSGE-VAR fewer shocks than observed variables

``estimation`` estimates a DSGE-VAR without ``bayesian_irf``, and the number of shocks is smaller than the number of observed variables. Dynare refuses: `When estimating a DSGE-Var, the number of shocks must be greater than or equal to the number of observed variables.`

**Fix**

Add shocks (``varexo``) until the shock count is at least the ``varobs`` count.
