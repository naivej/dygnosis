### E452: Nonstationary PAC target cannot be matched

A written model equation uses pac_target_nonstationary(NAME) when the named pac_model or pac_target_info block is missing. Dynare refuses at transform: `the pac_target_nonstationary operator does not match a corresponding 'pac_target_info' block`.

**Fix**

Add the missing PAC model or target-info block, or correct the operator name.
