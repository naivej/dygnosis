### E001: Parse error

The Dynare parser could not interpret the source. The diagnostic range points at the offending token or the nearest recoverable position. Dynare refuses with a generic bison `ERROR` at a location, or with `character unrecognized by lexer` when the source holds a double-quoted string.

**Warrant**

The editor names the missing construct and points at a usable range; Dynare's bison location is often the next token, and its lexer note names no range at all. When a reserved preprocessor symbol (`dsge_prior_weight`) is used outside a declaration, the message names the symbol and the positions that accept it, instead of Dynare's bare `unexpected DSGE_PRIOR_WEIGHT`.

**Common causes**

- Missing semicolon at the end of a declaration or equation
- Unbalanced parentheses, braces, or block keywords
- Malformed time subscript such as `y(1)` where `y(+1)` was meant
- A reserved keyword used as an identifier
- A reserved preprocessor symbol used where an expression is expected

**Fix**

Inspect the line cited and the line immediately preceding it. Dynare's preprocessor frequently flags the *next* line after a missing semicolon.
