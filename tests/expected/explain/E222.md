### E222: dsge_prior_weight missing from estimated_params

``estimation`` estimates the DSGE-VAR prior weight (bare ``dsge_var``) but ``dsge_prior_weight`` is not listed in ``estimated_params``. Dynare refuses: `When estimating a DSGE-VAR model and estimating the weight of the prior, dsge_prior_weight must be referenced in the estimated_params block.`

**Fix**

Add ``dsge_prior_weight`` to ``estimated_params``, or calibrate the weight with ``dsge_var=…``.
