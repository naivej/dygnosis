### E269: shocks correlation on mixed types

``shocks`` sets a correlation on a pair that is not both exogenous or both observed endogenous. Dynare refuses: `shocks: setting a correlation between '{a}' and '{b}'is not allowed; correlations can only be specified for exogenous or observed endogenous variables of same type`.

**Fix**

Use two exogenous names or two observed endogenous names.
