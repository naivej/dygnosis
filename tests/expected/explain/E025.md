### E025: Invalid model-local (`#`) variable

A model-local variable defined with `#` either reuses a declared `var`, `varexo`, or `parameters` name, or is used in an equation before its `#` definition. They refuse: `… has wrong type or was already used on the right-hand side. You cannot use it on the left-hand side of a pound ('#') expression`.

**Fix**

- Rename the model-local helper so it does not clash with a declared symbol
- Move the `#` definition above its first use
- Or remove the declaration if the name was meant to be only a model-local helper
